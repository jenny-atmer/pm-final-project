# GTM Launch Plan, Riverty business-unit case

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Pay-after-you-decide invoice (Major Project): the only thing that makes this more than a Riverty-branded debit card, |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Get existing Riverty app users who buy online with deferred payment to make their first card purchase at a non-partner shop within 21 days of approval, without reducing their partner-checkout usage.
- **Target audience:** Phase 0: 5000 invited friends-and-family testers, for readiness only.
Phase 1: consented Riverty app users with 5–9 Riverty transactions in the last 12 months, no arrears (thresholds set by Risk), size 50000, with a random 10% holdout.
Multi-provider status comes from the onboarding question and a short in-app survey to a random sample of invited users. It is used for analysis only.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, new product (credit card) entering a market with huge competition

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app (card tab, application, first-use prompt).**
2. **Owned: Owned email, including transactional emails (invoice, reminder, refund), to consented users only.**
3. **Owned: Partner comms (one-pager, owner named).**

## Enablement & Assets
email, in-app onboarding/application, one pager for partners, video demo, internal training material (support etc).Legal-approved product name, claims and terms; plain-language SCHUFA and fee disclosure (the 2023 interviews flagged both as gating); a tester feedback form.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns the plan and post launch metrics, marketing owns in-app copy, email, demo video, legal owns: name, claims, SCHUFA and fee copy, terms, Partner team: partner comms
- **Timeline:** Legal approves → Risk sets limits → live card issued → Phase 0 exit met → partner-checkout tagging live → support trained → platform capacity confirmed → 20 Nov 2026 rules checked. Then tranche 1 → weekly checks → activation read-out 21 days after the last approval → repeat-use read-out 30 days after that → go/no-go on wave 2.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** first card purchase at a non-partner shop creating an invoice, within 21 days of approval.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** other Riverty payment methods usage go down
- **Likely post-launch decision:** iterate
