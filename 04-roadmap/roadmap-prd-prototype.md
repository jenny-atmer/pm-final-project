## Your strategic anchors
- **Persona (M2), who are you solving for?:** Lena, 32, Cologne: a salaried, phone-first shopper on a middling income with no financial cushion, who uses BNPL on most non-trivial purchases and takes whichever option appears at checkout.
- **Primary success metric (M3), your leading indicator:** activation rate among approved users, with 70% as the target.
- **Moment of misery (M2), the specific friction blocking the goal:** the payment step where Riverty isn't offered. Lena is at a checkout or a till, she wants to pay only after she has decided to keep the item, and the option that would let her do that isn't there. She taps whichever BNPL option appears, usually Klarna or PayPal, or pays with an existing card.
- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Kill if activation falls below about 50%, or if the volume cannibalized from integrated checkout costs more margin than the card earns.
